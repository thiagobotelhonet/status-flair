# Code of conduct

Status Flair follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, reviews or any other project space.

If someone's behavior is a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their GitHub profile ([@thiagobotelhonet](https://github.com/thiagobotelhonet)). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules.
